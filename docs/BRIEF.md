# Project Brief: AeroBMSCE Website Revamp

## 1. Idea & Purpose
A revamped, ultra-fast, desktop-first website for **AeroBMSCE**, the student aeromodelling and aircraft-building club operating out of Propel Lab–3 at BMS College of Engineering, Bangalore (established in 2012). Built specifically for prospective members, competition judges, research mentors, and prospective industry/institutional sponsors who want to see what the club builds, its engineering pedigree, and how it performs internationally.

## 2. Visitor's One Primary Action
- **Primary Call to Action**: **Partner with Us** (Direct outreach to sponsor, collaborate, or provide technical ecosystem backing).
- **Secondary Action**: Join the club / Explore recruitment.

## 3. Aesthetic References & Benchmark Inspiration
- **linear.app**: Calm spacing, razor-sharp typography scale, confident restraint, disciplined micro-interactions, and dark technical depth.
- **spacex.com**: Full-bleed hardware photography that commands attention with minimal explanatory text; engineering speaks for itself.
- **teenage.engineering**: Unapologetic identity, playful yet rigorously precise, functional instrumentation aesthetic, distinct from generic web layouts.

## 4. Antipatterns & What We Dislike
- **Banned Look**: Any generic startup template — centered purple gradient hero, generic "Transform Your Future" headlines with dual pill buttons, and rows of identical soft rounded cards. It lacks identity and says nothing about aerospace engineering.

## 5. Tone & Personality
- **Feels like (3 words)**: `precise`, `bold`, `engineered`
- **Must NEVER feel like (3 words)**: `template`, `corporate`, `cute`

## 6. Color Direction & Palette Intent
- **Base Neutral**: Deep dark neutral / aerospace void (near-black or deep stratospheric navy).
- **Text & Contrast**: Crisp off-white / technical paper white for high-contrast legibility.
- **Single Signal Accent**: High-visibility aviation signal accent (Aviation Orange or Signal Flight Yellow, derived from AeroBMSCE branding and flight safety vernacular).
- **Hard Rule**: No generic AI purple gradients or terracotta/cream defaults.

## 7. Real Content & Artifacts Inventory
- **Brand Assets**: AeroBMSCE official emblem, header logo, tier badges (`images/logo.png`, `images/header-logo.png`, `images/icon-*.png`).
- **Partner & Institutional Logos**: DRDO, CSIR-NAL, Ansys, Altair, SolidWorks, Flow5, SimScale, Siemens, DRK Store, UAV Marketplace.
- **Hardware & Competition Imagery**: High-res build photography, CAD rendering (`cad-plane.png`), team photos, and field competition shots (`images/photo1.jpg` through `photo8.jpg`).
- **Legacy & Track Record**:
  - Founded in 2012 at Propel Lab–3, BMSCE.
  - 16+ years of collective lineage, 75+ projects, 50+ research papers, 20+ podium finishes.
  - Four dedicated flight divisions: Team Yaksha (International SAE), Team Blue Impulse (Aeromodelling), Team Flying Phantoms (Autonomous Drones), Team Vectors (Drone Racing).
  - Milestones spanning SAE Aero Design West (Fort Worth, Texas), SAE Aerothon, ADDC, NITK Drone Racing, and IIT Bombay Techfest.
  - Active R&D in indigenous flight controllers, ESCs, GPS-denied navigation, morphing wingtips, and confidential defense projects.
- **Pending/To Verify**: Exact individual member rosters, specific upcoming competition registration dates.

## 8. Signature Memorable Moment
- **Full-Bleed Hardware Hero**: A massive, full-viewport aircraft showcase with an orchestrated, scroll-linked flight trajectory across the view.
- **Flight-Log Achievements Timeline**: Competition results and historical podiums presented like an authentic aviation logbook / telemetry feed with dates, tail/model designations, flight categories, and podium rankings.
